### Maths & Physics for Games
- [Trigonometry](notes/trigonometry.md) - degrees & radians, SOH CAH TOA, atan2, the unit circle, angle conventions, angle → direction vectors, circles, waves, HSV rainbows
- [Vectors & Transforms](notes/vectors.md) - Godot's coordinate system, vector maths, delta, moving between positions, dot product & field of view, cross product, Basis & Transform3D, local vs global, look_at, follow cameras
- [Quaternions](notes/quaternions.md) - Euler angles & gimbal lock, Hamilton & Broome Bridge, axis-angle, rotating & combining, slerp, smooth turrets, flight camera
- [Physics](notes/physics_3d.md) - SUVAT, free fall, projectiles, air resistance, F = ma, _process vs _physics_process, Euler integration error, torque, inertia, Godot physics bodies, layers, signals, spawners, joints, raycasts, gravity gun, turret & homing missile
- [Godot & XR Topics](notes/godot_xr_topics.md) - FOSS, signals, null safety, tweens, particles, Android SDK, OpenXR Vendors, XR scene hierarchy, passthrough, piano keys, pitch & audio pools

The slides are [Marp](https://marp.app/) markdown. Open them in VS Code with the Marp extension to present them or export to PDF. The diagrams are in [notes/images](notes/images/) and are generated by [make_diagrams.py](notes/images/make_diagrams.py).
